Privacy Policy

FlowDojo  ·  AethericArts

Effective date: September 4, 2026
Last updated: September 4, 2026

Section 1

Introduction

AethericArts ("we," "us," or "our") operates FlowDojo, an app for learning flow arts — skill trees, video lessons, and practice tools for prop manipulation, beginning with staff. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using FlowDojo, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Section 2

Information We Collect

Account Information

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive from your identity provider:

  • A unique account identifier
  • Your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email")
  • Your name, if the provider shares it

You can use much of FlowDojo without an account; signing in exists to sync your data across devices.

Practice & Content Data

To provide the app's features, we store data you create:

  • Skill self-ratings and favorites
  • Practice routines and session history
  • Comments you post on skills, and votes you cast on videos

Camera & Video

FlowDojo can use your device's camera for delayed-playback self-review and for filming practice takes. These recordings stay on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot access them. Lesson and demo videos you download for offline use are cached on your device only.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files beyond what you explicitly record in-app
  • Biometric data
  • Advertising identifiers — FlowDojo has no ads and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs

Section 3

How We Use Information

  • To provide and sync your skill progress, routines, and favorites across devices
  • To display community content: comments you post appear with your account's display handle; video votes contribute to anonymous aggregate rankings
  • To moderate community content and keep it safe
  • To fix bugs and keep the service running

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Section 4

Service Providers

FlowDojo runs on infrastructure operated by third parties, which process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting (your account and practice data)
  • Bunny.net — video streaming; when you stream or download lessons, Bunny's CDN receives your IP address, as any content delivery network does
  • Apple / Google — sign-in identity providers, governed by their own privacy policies

Some lesson links open videos on YouTube; YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.

Section 5

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and practice data for as long as your account exists. To delete your account and its data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it within 30 days. Comments that were moderated (hidden) may be retained in a hidden state for audit purposes.

Data you keep only on your device — recordings, downloaded videos, local ratings without an account — is deleted whenever you delete it or uninstall the app.

Section 6

Children

FlowDojo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Section 7

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as FlowDojo evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
